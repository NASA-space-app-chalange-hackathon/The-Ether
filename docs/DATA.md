# Data

> All crew and herder data in The Ether is **synthetic**: it was generated by a seeded, deterministic script. No real person's data is used. Prototype for NASA Space Apps 2026. Not a medical device.

## Synthetic data: generator assumptions

Generator: `scripts/generate-mission.mts` (seed `2026`, run `npm run generate`). Output: `public/data/mission.json` and `public/data/steppe.json`. A unit test makes sure the committed JSON matches the generator exactly.

**Crew**: four fictional crewmembers with abstract callsigns ORION-A … ORION-D (Commander, Flight Engineer, Mission Specialist, Crew Medical Officer). Each has a personal set of resting values in typical healthy-adult ranges. **Mission**: 180 days of Mars transit, one check-in per day.

| Indicator | Daily model (all demo parameters) |
| --- | --- |
| Resting heart rate | personal mean 56–62 bpm, SD 2 |
| HRV (RMSSD) | personal mean 44–60 ms, SD 4 |
| Blood pressure | systolic 110–118 (SD 3.5), diastolic 68–76 (SD 2.5) mmHg; 3% of days not logged |
| Glucose | 86–94 mg/dL, SD 5; 3% missing |
| Temperature | 36.5–36.7 °C, SD 0.12; 3% missing |
| SpO₂ | 97–98 %, SD 0.5, rounded; 3% missing |
| Body mass | personal mass with slow in-flight loss of 0.006 kg/day, SD 0.25 kg |
| Sleep | 6.7–7.1 h, SD 0.5 h; quality (1–5) derived from duration plus noise |
| Mood, crew pulse | 1–5 scale, usually 4, with occasional ±1 days |
| PVT | mean RT 250–275 ms (SD 15); lapses Poisson(0.6–1.0) |
| Exercise | ~120–128 min/day, SD 12 |
| Radiation | 1.8 mSv/day (SwRI / MSL RAD cruise average) ± 0.06 |

**Story events**:

| Day | Event | How it's generated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40–41 | Solar particle event (all crew) | +22 mSv on day 40 and +6 mSv on day 41 (sheltered; demo magnitude); sleep −1.2 h and no exercise on those days |
| 60–75 | ORION-B hidden fatigue | sleep −1.55 h (≈ −22%), PVT mean RT +18%, lapses +2.5, with a 3-day ramp in and out. **Mood self-reports unchanged** |
| 80–100 | ORION-C cardiovascular creep | linear ramp to HR +13 bpm, systolic +24, diastolic +11 mmHg, HRV −12 ms by day 100; eases off after day 120 (treatment) |
| 100–180 | ORION-D skips exercise | 85% of days near 0 min, otherwise ~100 min |
| — | Prior career dose | A 20, B 0, C 30, D 10 mSv (fictional) |

**Steppe herder persona**: one fictional herder household, 30 winter days. Resting values: HR 70, BP 126/80, glucose 96, SpO₂ 96%. A cold snap from day 16 ramps BP up (+22/+10) and sleep down over 8 days. Glucose is logged on only ~70% of days (no meter at home every day).

## Other model assumptions (all demo parameters)

| Model | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Signal delay | Circular, coplanar orbits (Earth 1 AU / 365.25 d, Mars 1.524 AU / 687 d). In transit, the spacecraft spirals from Earth (day 0) to Mars (day 180), sweeping 120° of heliocentric angle. One-way delay = distance / c. Moon 384,400 km; ISS shown as "< 1 s". Sanity-checked against NASA's "up to 20 minutes one-way" on Mars. Labelled "approx." in the UI. |
| Missed exercise | Alert when 14-day adherence (minutes / 120, capped at 1; a missing log counts as 0) falls below 60%. |
| Quests | Routine quests verified from data: check-in (any reading that day), exercise ≥ 90 min, sleep ≥ 7 h, crew pulse ≥ 4. XP values and 1,000 XP per level are game-design parameters. Crew goal = share of routine quests done by the whole crew in the last 7 days; reward line at 80%. |
| Camera heart rate | Forehead ROI, 30 fps resample, 0.7–3.5 Hz band-pass, FFT peak. Quality = in-band power share at the peak (± 0.1 Hz) and its harmonic; below 0.3 the app asks for a manual pulse. Unit-tested on synthetic signals only; real-face accuracy must be checked by a person (target ± 10 bpm vs. a manual count). |
| PVT-lite | 60 s, random 2–10 s gaps; responses < 100 ms = false start; no response by 3 s = lapse; ≥ 3 valid trials needed to save. A 15 s demo run (1–3.5 s gaps) is offered and labelled. |
| Steppe care card | The herder household, its 38 km distance to the soum hospital and the ~1 h 30 min travel time are fictional and labelled as illustrative. |
